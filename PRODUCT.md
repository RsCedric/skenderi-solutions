# Skenderi Solutions — Product

## What it is
Skenderi Solutions is a small IT services firm (Switzerland, German-speaking). It builds websites and web apps, sets up and maintains IT infrastructure (networks, devices, cloud, Microsoft 365), and handles support and security for SMEs.

## Audience
Owners and office managers of small and mid-sized Swiss businesses (5–80 staff) who need one reliable IT partner instead of several freelancers. They are not technical; they value reliability, clear prices, fast response and someone who answers the phone.

## What the website must do
Make the visitor trust the firm in the first viewport and book a free initial consultation (Erstgespräch). Secondary: show the services and how working together runs.

## Brand commitments
- Existing logo: geometric "S" drawn as a circuit trace with two node dots, Midnight Navy #0B1B3A + Signal Blue #3D7BFF (in ../Skenderi-Solutions-Logo). Keep the mark.
- Language: German (Swiss usage: "ss" instead of "ß").
- Tone: direct, calm, competent. No buzzwords.

## Not true (do not invent)
No customer names, logos, testimonials, certifications, prices, response-time guarantees or headcounts. Placeholder contact data must be marked as such.

## Tech
Built with the owner's usual stack: Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, React Three Fiber / three.js, lucide-react, sonner.
